ABOUT CAMPUS QUEUE

Why we built a queue system for a campus

Every semester, students lose hours standing outside administrative offices for things that take a staff member two minutes to process. Campus Queue replaces the physical line with a virtual one: generate a token from anywhere, watch your position update, and walk in only when you're close to being called.

Departments manage their own queues independently. Staff can call the next student, skip someone who hasn't shown up, or mark a request as completed, and every action is logged for reporting and accountability.

For Students

One active token at a time, live position, and a full history of past visits.

For Staff

A simple counter view: call, skip, complete — plus daily stats for the department.

For Admins

Manage users, departments and services, and see reports across the whole campus.
